## Intent

A user manual for the Overtime Calculator app, in chapters so the app can link
to one: Salary and hours, Importing attendance, History and your payslip, End
of Service, Settings. Each chapter has its own title card. It shows what to
tap and what the numbers mean: the salary card, the month, the day sheet
(hours and minutes, lunch comes off, Friday and holiday, Medical leave),
import, the month filing itself in History, checking a payslip, End of
Service, and the Settings that match the company (30 days, shift times).
