## Intent

A how-to for getting your daily attendance out of the company portal and into
the Overtime Calculator app. The viewer should finish it able to do it alone:
log in, Request Lists, Daily Attendance Report, pick From and To dates,
Search, take the screenshots (down the table to the Total row, plus one
scrolled sideways at the bottom), then Import in the app, see "Matches the
report", and Fill in the days. Plain, calm, confident; short sentences.

## Assets

- ../../../glass-ot-green (app source) — the real app screens are captured from
  it with invented figures, never from a real account.
- User's screen recording of the portal — reference for the steps and menu
  names ONLY. Never shown: it contains the worker's name, photo, employee code
  and the company.

## Customizations

- English voiceover (generated), with large on-screen captions so it works muted.
- App screens shown inside a phone outline; the portal redrawn as neutral mock-ups.
- A light music bed under the voice.
- Look: the app's own design (green glass theme, its fonts and colours).

## Notes

- PRIVACY, hard rule: no company name, no logo (neither the company's nor
  its HR system's), no real name, photo, employee code, department or job title,
  no real times. Mock data only: "Worker name", code 00000, invented dates.
- Portal wording that may appear (generic labels): Dashboard, Request Lists,
  Daily Attendance Report, Employee Attendance Report, Attendance Date From,
  Attendance Date To, Search, First In, Last Out, Work Hrs, Status, Total,
  "Showing 1 to 31 of 31 entries".
- Watched inside the app from a "Watch how" link; hosted on a public GitHub
  help repo that contains nothing personal.
