Privacy policy

Overtime Calculator (Android app com.john3004.overtimecalculator and its web version). Last updated 3 October 2026.

Without an account

Everything you enter stays on your device: your pay, hours, saved months, payslip figures and photos, End of Service details, reminders and any worker names, employee numbers or employer names you type. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Attendance screenshots

Screenshots you import are read on your device. They are never uploaded. The app never signs in to your company's systems and never asks for that password.

With an account (optional)

You can sign in with an emailed code or with Google. If you sign in, the app stores:

  • your email address, to send you sign-in codes;
  • a copy of the data you entered in the app (listed above, payslip photos included), so you can use it on another phone or on the web.

This data is kept by Supabase, our database provider, in its Mumbai (India) region. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the database only lets your account read or change your data. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

In the app or the web app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account and synced data. Your account and everything stored with it online are deleted at once. See how to delete your account. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Other services

  • Help videos and the web app are served by GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address as any web server does.
  • The Android app may show Google Play's own rating card, run by Google.

The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
