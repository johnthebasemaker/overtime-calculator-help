Overtime Calculator

An Android and web app for workers in Saudi Arabia. It works out your monthly pay and overtime the way your company calculates it, checks your payslip, and estimates your End of Service.

What it does

  • Enter your salary once, then your hours each day, lunch included. Fridays, holidays, absences and medical leave are counted for you.
  • Import a month at once from screenshots of your attendance report, read on your phone.
  • Every finished month is saved to History, where you compare it with your payslip and see any shortfall.
  • End of Service, leave pay and air ticket, worked out under the Saudi Labour Law.
  • English and Arabic. Works offline. Signing in is optional and lets you use your data on another phone or on the web.
