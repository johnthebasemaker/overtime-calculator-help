Delete your Overtime Calculator account

You can delete your account and all data synced with it at any time, from the app or from the web.

Steps

  1. Open the app, or the web app at johnthebasemaker.github.io/overtime-calculator-web and sign in with your email.
  2. Open Settings (the green gear), then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and synced data and confirm.

What is deleted

Your account (email address) and every item synced with it are deleted from our database immediately. Nothing is kept.

Data stored on your own phone is not touched; remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Can't sign in?

Email johnsonandrew.j@outlook.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete the account for you.
